What happens when seeing clearly becomes hard?

We are increasingly asking AI to write our code, read our documents, research for us, recommend what to do, make decisions, and eventually to act.

As the volume and complexity of that work grows, more of it will sit beyond what an ordinary person can independently verify. The system may be doing useful work while the human overseeing it understands less and less of what actually happened.

At what point does assistance become dependence, and dependence become loss of agency? That possibility scares me.

I don’t think making the model itself safer is sufficient. Safety also depends on how these systems are deployed: what authority they receive, who is using them, what can go wrong at scale, and whether meaningful human oversight still exists.

That is the part of AI safety I’m increasingly interested in — AI technical governance, scalable oversight, and AI control.
